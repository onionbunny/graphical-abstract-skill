# SYNTHETIC SELF-TEST MATERIAL (fictional, not a formal case)

## sec.1
A CsPbI3 perovskite film was deposited by spin-coating and integrated into a
planar solar cell device. The study asks whether additive engineering raises
power conversion efficiency.

## sec.2
Reference films use no additive; optimized films use 2 mol% MACl. Devices were
measured under AM1.5G illumination. Efficiency values are in data.csv:
reference 21.4 %, optimized 24.8 %.

## sec.3
MACl additive improves film crystallinity, which raises device efficiency.
