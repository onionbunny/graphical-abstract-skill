# SYNTHETIC SELF-TEST MATERIAL (fictional, not a formal case)

## sec.1
A549 lung carcinoma cells were used to profile the dose-response of a fictional
investigational drug, Compound Y. The study asks how steeply cell viability falls
as Compound Y concentration increases.

## sec.2
Cells were treated for 48 h with Compound Y at 0, 1, 3, 10 and 30 uM. Viability
was measured by a luminescent ATP assay and normalized to the 0 uM vehicle
control. Mean values and SD (n=3) are in data.csv.

## sec.3
Viability declines monotonically with concentration: 100, 88.4, 67.2, 41.3 and
12.6 percent. The midpoint of the decline lies between 3 uM and 10 uM.

## sec.4
These data describe a single-dose-response curve consistent with concentration-
dependent cytotoxicity; an IC50 is reported qualitatively rather than fitted here.
