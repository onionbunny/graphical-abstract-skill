# SYNTHETIC SELF-TEST MATERIAL (fictional, not a formal case)

## sec.1
HT-1080 fibrosarcoma cells were used as the ferroptosis model. GPX4 is expressed
in HT-1080 cells and guards membranes against lipid peroxidation.

## sec.2
Cells were treated with 10 uM Compound X for 24 h. Viability was quantified by
CCK-8 absorbance (CCK-8 viability assay).

## sec.3
Compound X directly binds and inhibits GPX4. Viability values (mean +/- SD, n=3)
are provided in data.csv: control 92.1 +/- 3.2 %, Compound X 41.3 +/- 4.1 %.

## sec.4
These data support GPX4 inhibition-driven ferroptosis by Compound X.
