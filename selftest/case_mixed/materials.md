# SYNTHETIC SELF-TEST MATERIAL (fictional, not a formal case)

## sec.1
A polished metal substrate was used as the reaction surface. Oxygen radicals are
generated at the surface under ambient conditions.

## sec.2
Catalyst A was deposited as a thin coating on the substrate before each run.

## sec.3
The coating oxidizes the surface into an oxidized layer. The oxidized layer then
leaches a metal ion byproduct. Product yield values (mean +/- SD, n=3) are in
data.csv: control 88.2 +/- 2.7 %, Catalyst A 37.6 +/- 5.1 %.

## sec.4
The leached ion degrades the final product, so Catalyst A lowers stable output.
